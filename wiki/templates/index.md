# templates

- [Capability name](capability.md) — Author an existing capability with its purpose, real interfaces, connections, and evidence.
- [Outcome or finding](field-report.md) — Record an outcome, its evidence, the original author, and implications for future work.
- [Journey name](journey.md) — Author a complete path a user takes across several parts, with the connections that exist and the gaps.
- [Procedure name](runbook.md) — Author a repeatable procedure with prerequisites, verification, recovery, and provenance.
